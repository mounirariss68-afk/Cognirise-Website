# Cognirise hero film production notes

## Director's treatment

- **Format:** 16:9, 1920 × 1080 visual master, composed around a crop-safe central field for the website's asymmetric portrait-leaning window.
- **Visual arc:** complex violet, magenta, and coral signals gather inside warm architectural light; deep-navy structures give the flow direction; the signal crosses a clear threshold and opens into confident forward motion.
- **Camera language:** one continuous, controlled push through the architectural field. No hard cuts, glitches, stock footage, dashboards, or generic technology motifs.
- **Typography:** exact DOM-rendered Comfortaa phrases sit above the visual master in the website. This keeps every word crisp, selectable by assistive technology, and independent of generated imagery.
- **Loop:** the visual master is a 14.5-second seamless ambient loop at its native 24 fps cadence. Two separately generated eight-second shots continue the architectural signal journey at normal speed, with a 1.5-second cyclic finish returning the second shot to the opening composition. The complete message cycle is 30 seconds across six timed phrases.
