COGNIRISE LINKEDIN PULSE / EDITION 01

Included assets: three employee profile headers and six square post examples.
Publish-ready PNGs are provided at 2400 x 2400 for posts and 3160 x 790 for headers.
Self-contained, editable SVG sources are also included and do not depend on Canvas or web-hosted artwork.

POST TYPOLOGY
1. Point of view — a decisive position. One idea, no statistic required.
2. Insight / data — one sourced signal with an explicit source line.
3. Capability — explain one offer or operating move; lead with the work.
4. Case / outcome — only approved, attributable and permissioned proof.
5. People / culture — show practice and team perspective without unapproved portraits.
6. Event / announcement — only confirmed dates, places and releases.

HEADER OVERLAY GUIDANCE
LinkedIn overlays the lower-left portion of the banner with the profile photo.
The publish-ready PNG headers are clean and contain no dashed guide.
Editable SVG header sources retain the dashed composition guide; remove it before making a new final export.
Keep critical copy out of the lower-left 460px and at least 60px inside all edges.
