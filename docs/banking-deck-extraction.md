## Slide 1 — One bank. Three levels of AI value

**The value ladder**

- **L1 — Individual Assistants:** “AI assistants for every employee.” Every banker gets an assistant that drafts, summarises, searches policy and prepares meetings. It states a **+30% productivity gain target** and **8 weeks** time to value.
- **L2 — Agentic Functions:** “AI-powered domain processes.” AI takes over whole steps inside credit, operations, compliance and servicing — with human gates. It states a **+50% productivity gain target** and **3–6 months** time to value.
- **L3 — Agentic Enterprise:** “Autonomous agents at scale.” Agents run end-to-end processes, decide inside policy, and continuously optimise the outcome. It states a **+70% productivity gain target** and **6–12 months** time to value.
- Shared statement: “The ambition at every level is the same: additional capacity created without additional hiring.”

## Slide 2 — Six areas where we can create value with AI

1. **Credit & Lending:** AI reads the file, scores it against credit policy and drafts the decision so analysts spend time on judgement rather than extraction. Example use cases: RM meeting preparation and client briefs; credit-file and collateral summarisation; automated pre-screening and risk tiering.
2. **Risk & Fraud:** Models watch transactions and portfolios continuously, ranking what deserves human review instead of flooding analysts with alerts. Example use cases: real-time transaction anomaly detection; AML alert pre-screening and prioritisation; portfolio stress testing on demand.
3. **Operations & Process:** Agents remove manual touchpoints from middle and back office so people handle exceptions. Example use cases: onboarding and document verification; straight-through processing in the workflow engine; automated reconciliation and reporting.
4. **Customer & Sales:** Customers receive the right message at the right moment and bankers receive context before the conversation. Example use cases: AI voice and chat servicing; next-best-action recommendations; churn prediction and retention offers.
5. **Engineering & IT:** AI writes, reviews and tests code and reads legacy code so delivery can accelerate without more headcount. Example use cases: coding assistants; automated test and regression generation; legacy-code analysis and modernisation.
6. **Compliance & Regulation:** The regulatory corpus becomes searchable and self-monitoring, with answers traced to source. Example use cases: regulation and internal-policy search; circular and rule-change impact analysis; KYC and regulatory-reporting automation.
