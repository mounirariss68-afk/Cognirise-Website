# Education industry presentation

## Reference and decisions

The Public Sector, Telecoms and Energy pages are the presentation references.
Education uses their shared `IndustryEditorial` renderer and network hero variant:
native Comfortaa headline and Inter introduction beside an angular image, warm
paper background, navy opportunity and closing CTA, ruled pressure lists,
capability grid, evidence tables and source rows. At narrow widths the text comes
before the angular image. The rejected screenshots are not design targets.

There is no local menu, floating hero card, illustration caption, supporting
illustration, independent tab system or Education case-study rail. Other industry
rails are left for the separate consolidation work. No Financial Services changes
are imported from concurrent work.

## Delivery boundaries

The same view renders compiled fallback and governed published Education editions.
Requested-market projection is applied to the payload; protected previews pass
their requested market explicitly instead of inheriting the visitor's selection.
Configured loading/unavailable behavior remains authoritative.

Current Education media is one reviewed campus hero with an immutable version pin.
The retired supporting scenes remain available to historical records, not the
current presentation. Reconciliation appends a known-authority successor rather
than rewriting publication history, and the targeted publication command affects
Education only.
