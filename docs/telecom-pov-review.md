## Numeric decisions

No exact supplied figure was substantiated as a reported operator outcome. A focused external search on 2026-10-07 found a Netcracker vendor paper reporting **time to resolve complex B2B billing issues**, not the attachment’s **share resolved autonomously**. It is not used as substitute evidence:

The adjacent label for every retained performance figure is **Illustrative KPI target**. Each target has a metric definition, denominator or unit, relative direction, comparison basis and discovery/pilot caveat in the single metric registry.

Retained target hypotheses: cost per resolved contact 30–50% reduction; sales cycle 20–30% reduction; churn rate 10–25% reduction; manual data-entry effort 40% reduction; digital sales 30–50% increase; confirmed leakage rate 40–60% reduction; confirmed fraud loss rate 30–50% reduction; roaming settlement time 20–30% reduction; wholesale dispute rate 50% reduction; bad debt rate 20–35% reduction; approved insight time 60–80% reduction; IT restoration time 60–80% reduction; integration duration 50% reduction; release cycle 70% reduction; report preparation effort 80% reduction; ARPU 5–12% increase; bundle attachment 15–25% increase; device carrying cost 20–30% reduction. These are ambitions requiring commercial approval, not industry benchmarks.

All other supplied figures remain unresolved in the private source inventory, including hidden descriptions, chips, flagship assumptions, outcomes rows and adaptation impacts. No silent conversion to observed evidence is authorised.

Specific unresolved issues:
- “22% conversion rate” versus “22% higher conversion”: absolute conversion and relative uplift conflict; neither displayed.
- Relative NPS / app NPS percentages: NPS is percentage of promoters minus detractors; change should be percentage points, not a relative percent. No original magnitude displayed.
- Universal $10M recovery, $5M roaming loss, $10K intervention threshold and $38B industry fraud: missing scale, scope or exact source.
- 3× lead conversion / decisions / agent deployments: ambiguous baseline and quality definitions.
- 70% autonomous dispute resolution and 80% employee query handling: eligibility, repeat contacts and authority need definition.
- RAFM 2× cycle speed, 30% opex and combined leakage/fraud reduction: no common denominator and overlap not reconciled.
- Other numeric claims (24/7, 100% quality coverage, 50+ sources, 100+ systems, 1000+ links, millions of interactions) are unsupported scope/product assertions, not KPI targets.
- Fixed 30/60-day promises, week-by-week schedule and 6–12 month enterprise scale conflict or lack delivery evidence; replaced by scope-dependent milestones.

## Authority and substantive corrections

Universal reasoning/acting/learning is replaced by tested workflows and approved changes. More autonomy is not an objective. Blanket high-risk HITL is replaced by action-specific permissions, reach, reversibility, approval, intervention, audit and recovery. Credits and settlements need financial limits; credit/collections need policy, fairness and hardship review; SIM suspensions and routing need authorised fraud/NOC handoffs; tariff changes need commercial/regulatory review; rating patches and infrastructure changes need tested change control and rollback.

Network telemetry may inform enterprise decisions; NEP/NOC retains network control. No autonomous routing, millisecond blocking or independent network remediation. QoS pricing is only a regulated adaptation hypothesis. Existing systems remain usable without a new unified lake, and orchestration is not training. Partner ownership and canonical platform definitions are unchanged.
