## Non-numerical supplied claims: treatment

- The three-level ladder and its domain/use-case examples are retained as **illustrative workflow possibilities**, with owners, readiness, KPIs and decision boundaries added. L3 is explicitly not unrestricted autonomy, not synonymous with CogniOS levels and not mandatory for every workflow.
- The six domains and seven voice journeys are retained as a discoverable scope map. KPI directions are retained but no numerical performance result is asserted.
- “Voice and data never leave the bank,” claims of automatic data-residency compliance, and “built for SOC 2, GDPR, SAMA and CBUAE” are excluded. On-premise deployment alone cannot establish the whole deployment/integration data path or compliance.
- Lupitor’s action claims are recast as **platform descriptions**. The site distinguishes its possible platform contribution from Cognirise’s journey redesign, integration, evaluation, operating model and governance work.
- Ekimetrics’ contribution is described only as vendor-positioned financial-services data/measurement/customer-intelligence capability. Exact ROI, lifetime-value and time-to-impact claims are excluded.

## Public-copy control rules

1. The only numbers in Banking POV copy are source-attributed and locally qualified: the UK survey, DBS company report and FSB consultation’s practice count.
2. The UAE market record contains UAE-specific CBUAE source association and no Saudi Arabia/SAMA/Kingdom reference.
3. No external or partner example is presented as Cognirise work. Existing selected-work membership, content, classifications, visuals, order and links are frozen by the case receipt in `scripts/cms/output/banking-published-baseline.json`.
4. The product/partner links are public source links, not claims of a formal alliance or endorsement.
5. The CBUAE guidance is used as operational context. The page does not claim certification, approval, legal compliance or data residency based solely on a deployment pattern.
