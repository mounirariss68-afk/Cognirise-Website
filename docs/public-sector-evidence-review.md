## Purpose and method

This review reconciles the four supplied market editions without adding claims to
the copy. The attachment structure is preserved exactly:

1. Hero / Industry Perspective
2. Industry Opportunity
3. Operating Pressures
4. Value Domains and Capabilities
5. Representative Applications
6. Critical Perspective
7. Market Context
8. Supporting Evidence / Source Trail
9. Relevant Next Action

Line spans below are inclusive and refer to the four attachments. `verified`
means that the checked URL supports the mapped proposition. `partial` means
that it supports a narrower proposition. `unverified` means that no bounded
source established the proposition. A missing URL is represented as an empty
value in the ledger and is a publication blocker, never a placeholder.

## Draft blockers and publication rule

The ledger deliberately retains unresolved items rather than filling gaps:

1. All twelve shared application mappings now have explicit institution,
   jurisdiction and bounded `sourceUrls`. The exact 27 attachment rows are
   retained unchanged, with three clearly marked shared-application rows
   added for GOV.UK Chat, Consult and Estonia OPSI. The separate
   planning-service document-digitisation example still has no located
   institution/source.
2. The UAE Phase 1 results, UAE PASS service/entity totals, TAMM service
   total and legal-scope details require narrower primary evidence.
3. Saudi Nafath, Absher and BALSAM figures require dated records with
   denominators.
4. Türkiye's Presidential Decree row now has the dated 28 March 2025
   Resmî Gazete issue URL, but the issue text was not exposed by the bounded
   fetch and needs legal-text confirmation. The e-Devlet, MERNİS and BSYS
   metric bundles also need current official reports.
5. EU EUDI timing, the 93-percent benchmark figure and EU20 language
   figures require exact table/article citations.
6. The universal containment proposition in the critical-perspective copy
   remains unresolved.

No source URL is invented, and no `partial` or `unverified` proposition is
promoted as an independently verified outcome. The content should remain in
review until these blockers are either resolved with an authentic source or
the relevant wording is narrowed or removed.
