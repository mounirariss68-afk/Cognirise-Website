# Cognirise Industry Image Direction

## Shared world
A premium, near-future architectural landscape rather than a literal workplace. Monumental warm-ivory stone and satin concrete are counterweighted by deep midnight-navy structures, with selective translucent glass, dark reflective surfaces, and small natural elements. Every scene feels substantial, credible, calm, and meticulously built.

## Light, camera, and composition
Soft dawn or late-afternoon light creates warm highlights, long controlled shadows, and gentle atmospheric depth. Architectural wide-angle perspectives establish scale without distortion. The square frame has a clear foreground entry, a strong spatial anchor, and a luminous destination; important objects remain comfortably inside clean edges.

## Pulse flow
Violet, magenta, coral, and restrained warm-orange paths are integrated into floors, channels, apertures, and gateways. They express movement and exchange through a few purposeful routes—not decorative neon, uncontrolled spectacle, or a blanket of light.

## Quality bar
Photorealistic cinematic raster rendering; plausible geometry; premium stone, metal, glass, water, and landscape materials; disciplined visual density; clear sector metaphor at a glance; no accidental text, marks, or malformed objects.

## Sector concepts
- **Banking:** Separate value paths pass through nested governed apertures and emerge as a trusted, orderly exchange beside protected repositories.
- **Defense:** Concentric protective layers coordinate and shelter a calm central core, expressing readiness, redundancy, and continuity without combat imagery.
- **Education:** Knowledge routes exchange across connected study alcoves and rise through successive gateways, expressing progression and mastery without a literal classroom.
- **Energy & Resources:** Geological terrain, water, regulating gates, and circular chambers turn a raw source flow into controlled infrastructure and distribution.
- **Travel & Hospitality:** Distinct journey paths converge through graceful routing arches into a warm welcome threshold, with onward gateways suggesting attentive service.

## Exclusions
No captions, letters, numbers, logos, flags, insignia, dashboards, generic icons, robots, identifiable people, uniforms, weapons, combat, military hardware, vehicles, aircraft, branded institutions, literal stock-photo scenes, cartoons, vector styling, or clipped focal objects. Avoid obvious shorthand such as coins, padlocks, graduation caps, oil derricks, hotel beds, and landmark replicas.
