## Findings Log
### Baseline
- Current starting labels mix scale (organization-wide, one workflow), volume (multiple opportunities), specialist solution concepts (human–agent work design, handover authority) and operating state (live evidence).
- The existing list maps almost one-to-one to methods; this is a baseline to challenge, not evidence of real demand.
- Strategy already purchased does not by itself prove a business case, tested solution or delivery readiness.

## Conflicts & Open Questions
- Wharton, Cisco and McKinsey use different populations, questions and value definitions; do not compare their headline ROI/measurement percentages. [@wharton-genai-adoption-2025] [@cisco-ai-readiness-index-2025] [@mckinsey-gcc-state-ai-2025]
- NBER and Alibaba field studies establish context-specific effects, not a universal return. Historical NBER draft has different estimates than later publications; omit headline percentages. [@nber-generative-ai-at-work-2023] [@alibaba-customer-service-field-experiment-2025]
- London CIV explicitly separates strategy procurement from implementation, but strategy was recently initiated: no claim of completed consultant strategy or implementation success. [@london-civ-ai-automation-implementation-partner]
- Seven entries and their ordering are recommendations, not empirically validated demand segments. Specialist authority questions remain reachable without becoming compulsory for every buyer.

### Triangulated findings
- Investment needs include identifying a useful problem, comparing alternatives and building evidence; not all nonadopters need AI. [@oecd-ai-adoption-barriers] [@csiro-ai-project-selection] [@gap-sme-uk-dsit-2026] [@gap-sme-oecd-2025]
- Delivery entails integration, lifecycle support, skills and ownership beyond an approved direction. [@govuk-ai-procurement-guidelines] [@dbt-ai3-delivery-partners] [@london-civ-ai-automation-implementation-partner]
- Work context, skill differences and workflow design matter to outcomes; separate operational need from choosing agents. [@nber-generative-ai-at-work-2023] [@alibaba-customer-service-field-experiment-2025] [@mbs-ai-redesign-of-work-2026]
- A pilot, a stable live service and evidence that it can scale are different assets, not interchangeable milestones. [@rand-ai-failure-roots-2024] [@cisco-ai-readiness-index-2025] [@mckinsey-gcc-state-ai-2025]
- Adoption, workforce confidence and value measurement are substantive regional concerns, not just technical deployment. [@redhat-uae-2025] [@pwc-uae-workforce-2025] [@pwc-saudi-ai-value-2026] [@kpmg-uae-future-of-work-2024]
