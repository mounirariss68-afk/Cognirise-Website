### Description

This contract has been awarded under the Crown Commercial Services DPS Framework (RM6200).Contract is for the provision of a AI delivery partner to support the creation of central AI infrastructure for the department. This capability is required to meet ambitions set out in the Prime Minister&apos;s AI Action Plan and the subsequent departmental demand for AI. The maximum contract value is approx. £3m for the 1st year, other optional extensions are subject to budget and funding availability total £6m.
