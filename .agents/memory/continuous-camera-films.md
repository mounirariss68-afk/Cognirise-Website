---
name: Continuous camera films
description: How to extend a short-clip generator into a convincing forward architectural journey without substituting static artwork.
---

For a long architectural hero, condition each new shot on the preceding shot's clean final frame and describe both the next physical movement and the specific choice the camera makes. A moving light line over a still, or a loop of one short passage, does not convey travel or decision-making. Inspect intermediate frames and joins, not just the opening and final poster.

**Why:** Short image-to-video shots can form a credible long route when their camera views are connected. Even with a no-text prompt, the model can unexpectedly add plaques at a junction; a single contaminated segment should be replaced rather than propagated as the next shot's source. CPU-only realistic 3D renders were impractically slow here, while fast unlit renders looked too dark for the approved bright architecture.

**How to apply:** Establish a route sequence with visible alternatives, pauses, clear commitments and sustained forward parallax. Inspect every segment before using its final frame as the next reference, reject accidental text, and verify the assembled film's actual visual motion and loop rather than only its duration, resolution or first/last pixel similarity.
