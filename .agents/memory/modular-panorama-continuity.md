---
name: Modular panorama continuity
description: How to preserve exact joins across independently reusable Pulse journey modules.
---

Any modular Pulse journey intended to assemble as a panorama must be created as one connected master composition first, then divided only through shared platform or conduit sections. Preserve the source segment widths proportionally and do not translate or scale individual modules in the assembled state.

**Why:** Matching themes, lighting, and a separate spine are not enough to make independently generated scenes feel connected. Exact physical joins require shared source pixels, and per-module emphasis transforms visibly pull those joins apart.

**How to apply:** Use one camera, baseline, lighting setup, platform, and signal route across the master. Split at intentional connector-only boundaries, verify that reassembly is pixel-identical, and express disclosure emphasis through tint, opacity, or overlays rather than geometry changes.
