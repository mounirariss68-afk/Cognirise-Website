---
name: Pulse artwork text hygiene
description: How to keep synthetic labels out of light architectural Pulse illustrations.
---

When a generated architectural image contains labels or pseudo-text, do not rely on a generic “remove all text” edit. Replace the text-bearing sheets, panels or signs with abstract architectural surfaces that contain only line networks, nodes and geometry, then inspect the final raster at full size.

**Why:** Direct erasure can preserve or regenerate plausible-looking nonsense labels, especially on layered blueprints and technical diagrams.

**How to apply:** Use semantic surface replacement for any public-facing Pulse raster with plans, gates, consoles or assurance panels. Check every final image visually before referencing it in the website or a deck.
