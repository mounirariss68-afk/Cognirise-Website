name: Team editorial style
description: Approved advisor biography and contribution writing style.
---

Advisor bios should be compact and fact-first: concrete roles, dates, operating scale and relevant career history. Follow with one concise contribution paragraph explaining the perspective brought to Cognirise, not generic praise.

**Why:** The owner explicitly preferred a supplied advisor profile over the earlier generic summary and requested the other advisor's biography in the same style.

**How to apply:** Preserve owner-supplied facts and distinguish historical figures from current ones. Use verified recognition wording rather than inflated rankings. Keep the existing portrait shape and page layout when revising copy.
