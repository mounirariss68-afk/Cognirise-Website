---
name: CMS editor simplicity
description: Approved direction for reducing CMS interaction complexity without weakening governance.
---

Keep ordinary editing compact, particularly People as one table with market ticks. Separate pending decisions from live availability, and move regional exceptions/history into secondary detail.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved simplifying the CMS after the People availability-card grid duplicated the table and exposed inconsistent workflows. They want fewer steps, not fewer capabilities.

**How to apply:** Preserve review, role/market authority, immutable revision/media history and recovery behind simpler controls. Do not interpret approval of usability remediation as approval to publish content or remove governance.
