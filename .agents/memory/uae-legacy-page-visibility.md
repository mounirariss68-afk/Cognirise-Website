---
name: UAE legacy page visibility
description: Why approved UAE website content remains publicly renderable before its CMS migration is complete.
---

The project owner expressly approved the existing UAE website pages for public viewing despite incomplete CMS migration. Keep the pre-existing compiled versions available for the UAE English edition when no immutable CMS release covers a route. Do not interpret this as permission to expose mutable CMS drafts, forge authenticated editorial actions, or declare a CMS release complete.

**Why:** Strict release-only route gating hid previously authored pages while the active release had no platform revisions and several other website sections had no published CMS equivalents. The immediate request was to restore the existing UAE site, not to invent publication history.

**How to apply:** Give actual immutable CMS releases precedence. Limit legacy delivery to already authored, known UAE routes and content; do not expand it to other markets or new draft content. Migrate those pages into the normal release flow separately, then remove the exception when coverage is complete.

Owner-confirmed launch contact details are an explicit exception to release precedence on the public contact page, across launch markets. Protected editorial previews retain CMS authority.

**Why:** The owner supplied replacement London and Amsterdam addresses and the support mailbox while CMS migration remained incomplete. Stale published contact details must not override these corrections.

**How to apply:** Keep this narrowly scoped to the confirmed contact details, preserve other offices and immutable history, and hand the corrected values back to governed publishing before removing the launch override.
