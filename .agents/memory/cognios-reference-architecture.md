---
name: CogniOS reference architecture
description: Durable content and interaction rules for presenting the CogniOS architecture.
---

Use the stable six-layer model in this order: Experience, Intelligence, Process, Knowledge, Integration, and Foundation, with AI Governance & Assurance and Platform Engineering & Operations as cross-cutting spines. Keep layer → component → detail exploration inside one continuous spatial stage on the CogniOS platform page; legacy architecture URLs may resolve into that in-page state, but do not recreate a separate explorer, modal, drawer, accordion, tabs, or generic card grid.

**Why:** The original source contains the intended structural hierarchy but also mixes in maturity labels, deployment guarantees, localisation promises, and other assertions that are not approved as production claims. A separate explorer and conventional UI containers made the hierarchy feel disconnected and harder to navigate.

**How to apply:** Preserve responsibilities, relationships, and the two-level drill-down. Expand selected layers and components spatially while keeping the other planes and both spines visible. On mobile, use vertical inline expansion. Frame components as a reference architecture, and omit unsupported status, sovereignty, compliance, language, deployment, recovery, and capability guarantees.
