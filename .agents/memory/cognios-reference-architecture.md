description: Durable content and interaction rules for presenting the CogniOS architecture.
---

Use the stable six-layer model in this order: Experience, Intelligence, Process, Knowledge, Integration, and Foundation. L0 is the complete system map: stack those six layers vertically, framed by AI Governance & Assurance (A) and Platform Engineering & Operations (B) as continuous vertical columns. Selecting an L0 layer reveals its L1 components as side-by-side boxes. Selecting an L1 component preserves the whole box group and opens L2 full-width beneath it, never to the right. Keep exploration inside one continuous spatial stage on the CogniOS platform page; legacy architecture URLs may resolve into that in-page state, but do not recreate a separate explorer, modal, drawer, accordion, tabs, or generic card grid.

**Why:** The original source contains the intended relationships but also mixes in maturity labels, deployment guarantees, localisation promises, and other assertions that are not approved as production claims. A horizontal six-layer L0 incorrectly changed the architecture hierarchy; the user explicitly clarified stacked L0 layers, A/B columns, side-by-side L1 components and below-group L2.

**How to apply:** Preserve responsibilities, relationships, and the two-level drill-down. Keep both vertical spines visible while L1 and L2 expand within the center stack. On mobile, retain the columns and use vertical inline component flow. Frame components as a reference architecture, and omit unsupported status, sovereignty, compliance, language, deployment, recovery, and capability guarantees.
