---
name: Image-led hero vertical contract
description: Vertical composition rule for Cognirise split heroes with copy beside a fixed-height image.
---

On desktop image-led heroes, treat the left column as two groups spanning the image height: Back plus the eyebrow at the top, and the complete narrative/action group at the bottom. Keep flexible white space between them. The final action should align with the image bottom. Do not stack every element from the top merely to align Back with the image.

**Why:** Top-stacking fixed the Back edge but collapsed the intentional white space and destroyed the established hierarchy. The homepage demonstrates the approved balance; IDAO requires the same structure while keeping its full “Innovate” through lifecycle action group intact.

**How to apply:** Use a full-height desktop copy column with a top group, flexible spacer, and bottom group. On mobile, remove the forced image-height behavior and return to natural stacked flow.
