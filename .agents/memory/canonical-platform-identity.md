---
name: Canonical platform identity
description: Governs how published platform content composes with the website’s fixed public platform ecosystem.
---

The public platform inventory owns each platform’s name, order, ownership classification and canonical route. Published CMS records may enrich approved descriptive copy for matched Cognirise-owned entries, but cannot add cards, duplicate partners or restore retired identities.

**Why:** Mutable published records can outlive a rename or partnership change. Treating them as the navigation authority can silently restore retired names, duplicate hand-authored partner entries or point visitors at dead routes.

**How to apply:** When platform CMS delivery or navigation changes, reconcile records against the canonical inventory by approved slug aliases. Keep partner narratives and ownership in their evidence-backed source, and preserve retired URLs only as redirects.
