---
name: Website page discovery
description: Why the CMS page menu uses a simple discovery view rather than the market-availability editor.
---

Website Pages is for finding governed landing-page documents and choosing an exact authorized market/language edition. It must not imply that finding a page creates a new public route or changes its publication state.

**Why:** The general content market matrix exposes availability and source-management controls. Editors needed a predictable page-finding entry point, not a second place where discovery could accidentally be confused with staged availability or live publication.

**How to apply:** Keep page-list actions navigational and derive visible documents and exact edition targets from the permission-filtered CMS APIs. Put editing, source/availability changes, and publication in their existing governed detail flows.
