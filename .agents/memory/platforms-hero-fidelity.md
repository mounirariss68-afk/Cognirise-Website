name: Platforms hero fidelity
description: Visual acceptance boundary for replacing the Platforms page CogniOS hero footage.
---

Retain the approved Platforms hero footage rather than substituting a procedural or image-derived 3D mesh that only approximates its layered CogniOS ecosystem.

**Why:** A geometrically exact turntable was rejected because its simplified surfaces, lighting, central structure, and overall identity were far from the approved cinematic raster artwork. Exact rotation alone does not justify a visual downgrade.

**How to apply:** Treat the current route-owned video as the visual baseline. Only propose replacing it when a candidate preserves the recognizable layered architecture, luminous central spine, depth, materials, and cinematic finish closely enough to be reviewed as an improvement rather than a reconstruction.
