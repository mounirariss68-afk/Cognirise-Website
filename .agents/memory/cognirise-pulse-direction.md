---
name: Cognirise Pulse direction
description: The approved visual and interaction direction for future Cognirise brand work.
---

Use Cognirise Pulse as the shared design language: an off-white architectural canvas, deep navy structure, violet–magenta–coral directional energy, Comfortaa display type, Inter body type, cinematic original imagery, angular crops, and editorial asymmetry. Flagship brand illustrations must feel dimensional, materially realistic, and art-directed; do not substitute cartoon-like flat vector drawings for premium 3D or cinematic imagery. Motion should use image reveals, directional signals and spatial shifts rather than generic technical motifs. Its interaction principle is controlled momentum: keep browser-native scrolling, scope continuous motion to the visible focal element, make reduced-motion content immediately visible, and avoid page-wide parallax, repeated reveal cascades, infinite “breathing,” scroll hijacking or pinned storytelling. Primary CTAs use one continuous navy or inverse surface with an integrated arrow and an always-visible violet–magenta–coral Signal Rail; never use a detached or inset white arrow tile. Domain content should become bespoke spatial representations—not pale card grids, empty numbered steppers or repeated accordion rows.

For Arabic and bilingual campaign work, Alexandria is the approved Arabic family alongside Comfortaa display and Inter utility text. Arabic leads the hierarchy and aligns right; bilingual English supports at reduced scale. Mirror type fields and spacing for RTL, but do not flip Pulse’s angular crops or directional currents merely to follow reading direction. Employee headers keep Arabic identity copy in the right-side field, clear of LinkedIn’s lower-left portrait overlay.

**Why:** The user approved Cognirise Pulse as the creative standard, then explicitly rejected the inset-arrow CTA, card-grid and thin-stepper regressions, grey-box treatments, and cartoonish flat skyline artwork as materially below that standard. A page-wide parallax and repeated reveal implementation also made the homepage feel stuck, unreliable and less intentional.

**How to apply:** Use this direction across Cognirise website pages, presentations, campaign assets and related brand materials unless the user explicitly asks for a new visual direction. Translate each content model into its own clear spatial composition. When a supplied reference uses a dark or neon treatment, preserve only its useful structure and storytelling; do not inherit that palette by default. Pulse imagery remains off-white-dominant unless the user explicitly requests a dark surface.
