name: Methodology journey preference
description: User-preferred exploration for the seven-situation methodologies selector.
---

The user prefers a left-to-right journey of seven situations, from exploring AI through recovering missing value, shown as one row on desktop. Their favored concept is one continuous architectural panorama rather than seven unrelated image cards. Keep the image fixed, unfiltered and undistorted: hovering or focusing a region should highlight that region, show its associated wording, and highlight the matching stop on the separate journey row beneath the image. Clicking commits the stop and updates the guidance below, without repeating the selected heading or image there. Mobile needs a readable alternative to seven compressed stops. “Choose a threshold” is the preferred journey heading.

**Why:** The user liked the continuous-image direction over unrelated image cards, but rejected several attempts at IDAO-style expansion because they either failed to expand the image or distorted its aspect ratio. They explicitly asked to restore the journey beneath the image and use highlighting and wording only; later they requested synchronized hover on the journey row. This remains a canvas exploration, not approval to integrate into the live website.

**How to apply:** When revisiting or integrating the selector, preserve the seven situations and their evidence-dependent method guidance. Treat the current preview as an exploration, not final approval to release; maintain a genuinely connected master image with no whole-image dark filter, keep the separate journey row visible, and verify highlight/focus and mobile selection before replacing the website.
