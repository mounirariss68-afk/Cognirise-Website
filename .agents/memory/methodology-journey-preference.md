They explicitly asked to restore the journey beneath the image and use highlighting and wording only; later they requested synchronized hover on the journey row. Once that worked, they approved the design for the methodologies page while expressly requiring its existing hero to remain.

Never replace the methodologies hero or its film when revising the selector beneath it.
