---
name: Editorial evidence joins
description: Preventing plausible but unrelated evidence from entering regional editorial drafts.
---

Join editorial evidence by the exact attachment claim and market, not by acronym, approximate title, array position, or a convenient available URL. Preserve the supplied evidence category unless a reviewer explicitly approves a change.

**Why:** Similar acronyms and institutional names can refer to unrelated programmes. A plausible citation can conceal a false attribution, and a draft blocker does not make that attribution acceptable.

**How to apply:** Check the ledger's section names and claim text against the actual attachment before consuming it. Require explicit application-to-source associations; unresolved attribution must stay unresolved rather than receive provisional institution names or arbitrary citations.
