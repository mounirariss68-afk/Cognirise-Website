# CLINIC NETWORK

## Bringing patient engagement under consent-aware control

Connect patient enquiries, care follow-up and campaign planning in one bilingual workspace. The platform enables a clinic network to coordinate engagement while keeping consent checks, clinical-data boundaries and human oversight visible.

## 01 / OBJECTIVE — The mandate

Create a consistent approach to patient engagement across the clinic network, from initial enquiry to follow-up, without allowing marketing access to clinical records.

## 02 / WORK — What changed

Built an Arabic–English platform combining enquiry handling, care follow-up and campaign management. Insurance-benefit campaigns use consent-scoped audiences, with oversight in a shared command centre.

## 03 / CONTROLS — How it stayed bounded

Consent checks and restricted targeting fields define campaign boundaries. A compliance ledger makes permissions visible. The platform is able to connect to multiple channels (app, web app, WhatsApp and voice) in an omni-channel approach with context transfer to human agents.

## 04 / IMPACT — What can be said

The platform makes engagement workflows and governance controls tangible for review and measures the impact it has on intended KPIs.
