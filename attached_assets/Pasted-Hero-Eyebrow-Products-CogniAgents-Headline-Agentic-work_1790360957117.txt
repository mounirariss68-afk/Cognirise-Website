The problem

Generic AI assistants answer questions, but they don't finish the work. Building agents from scratch takes months, and every team reinvents the same processes. Many pilots automate a broken process instead of a good one, and agents that act without oversight are a risk no compliance team will sign off on.

CogniAgents start from a proven way of running each function, then adapt it to yours.

What makes CogniAgents different

Best practices, pre-built
Each agent encodes how leading organisations run that function: the steps, the checks, the approvals and the KPIs. You start from a mature process, not a blank canvas.

Tailored to your business
Workflows, rules, approval thresholds, tone and terminology are configured to your policies. Agents adapt to your process instead of forcing you into theirs.

Agents that act, not just answer
CogniAgents read, decide and take action in your systems: they create tickets, update records, draft documents, route cases and trigger follow-ups.

Human in the loop, by design
You define exactly where people review, approve or override. Every decision is logged with its reasoning and the sources it used.

Grounded in your knowledge
Built on CogniBase, every agent works from your own documents, data and systems, with citations, so outcomes are explainable and auditable.

Sovereign and secure
Agents can be deployed in an EU cloud, in your own tenant or on premises, with open-weight models where required. Role-based permissions limit what each agent can see and do.

FAQ

Are these off-the-shelf or custom? Both. Each agent starts from a best-practice template and is then tailored to your processes, systems and policies.
Can agents take action in our systems? Yes, through CogniBase's integration layer, and always within permissions and approval rules you define.
How do we keep control? Human checkpoints, role-based permissions and full decision logs are built into every workflow.
How quickly can we start? A single-function pilot typically runs in weeks, starting with one process and clear KPIs.
Do you support the EU AI Act? CogniAgents are designed with transparency, human oversight and logging, which support obligations under the EU AI Act.
